### 💊 [Daily Wellness Scheduler](https://github.com/jacobdcook/daily-wellness-scheduler)
My most recent project! A comprehensive **full-stack web application** for managing wellness routines. Rebuilt with a modern Next.js frontend and FastAPI backend, it handles everything from supplement schedules to nutrition tracking and AI-powered coaching.

**Key Features**: 
- Smart scheduling for meals, workouts, and study blocks
- Nutrition tracking with barcode scanning & photo recognition
- Habit tracking with streaks and analytics
- Self-hostable with Cloudflare Tunnel & mobile-ready PWA

**Tech Stack**: `Next.js` `FastAPI` `React` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS` `Python`
